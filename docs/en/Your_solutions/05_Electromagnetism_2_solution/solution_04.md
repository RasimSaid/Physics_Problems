# 4. Magnetic Torque

## Problem

A rectangular loop of wire has dimensions:

- Length = 10 cm
- Width = 5 cm

The loop carries a current:

- I = 2 A

A uniform magnetic field is applied:

- B = 0.3 T

The magnetic field is parallel to the plane of the loop.

Find the magnitude of the magnetic torque on the loop.

---

# Given

```text
Length = 10 cm = 0.10 m
Width  = 5 cm  = 0.05 m
Current I = 2 A
Magnetic Field B = 0.3 T
Number of turns N = 1
```

---

# Formula

$$
\tau = N I A B \sin(\theta)
$$

Where:

- tau = magnetic torque
- N = number of turns
- I = current
- A = area of loop
- B = magnetic field
- theta = angle between magnetic moment and magnetic field

---

# Step 1: Calculate Area

$$
A = l \times w
$$

$$
A = 0.10 \times 0.05
$$

$$
A = 0.005 \text{ m}^2
$$

---

# Step 2: Determine Angle

Since the magnetic field is parallel to the plane of the loop:

$$
\theta = 90^\circ
$$

Therefore:

$$
\sin(90^\circ) = 1
$$

---

# Step 3: Calculate Torque

Substitute the values into the formula:

$$
\tau = (1)(2)(0.005)(0.3)(1)
$$

$$
\tau = 0.003 \text{ N m}
$$

---

# Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
\tau = 3.0 \times 10^{-3} \text{ N m}
}
$$

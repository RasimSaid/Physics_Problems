## 10. Force Field and Power

**Given:** $m = 0.5 \, \text{kg}$, $x = 5t^2 - t, y = 2t^3, z = -3t + 2$.

**Solution:**
* **Velocity ($\vec{v}$):** $\frac{d\vec{r}}{dt} = \mathbf{(10t-1, 6t^2, -3)}$
* **Momentum ($\vec{p}$):** $m\vec{v} = \mathbf{(5t-0.5, 3t^2, -1.5)}$
* **Acceleration ($\vec{a}$):** $\frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = \mathbf{(10, 12t, 0)}$
* **Force ($\vec{F}$):** $m\vec{a} = \mathbf{(5, 6t, 0)}$
* **Power ($P$):** $\vec{F} \cdot \vec{v} = 5(10t-1) + 6t(6t^2) = \mathbf{36t^3 + 50t - 5 \, \text{W}}$

## 11. Dynamics with Time-Dependent Force

**Given:** $m=3 \, \text{kg}, \vec{F} = (15t, 3t-12, -6t^2)$. $\vec{v}_0=(2,0,1)$, $\vec{r}_0=(5,2,-3)$.

**Solution:**
* **$\vec{a}(t)$:** $(5t, t-4, -2t^2)$
* **$\vec{v}(t)$:** $\int \vec{a} dt + \vec{v}_0 = \mathbf{(\frac{5}{2}t^2+2, \frac{1}{2}t^2-4t, -\frac{2}{3}t^3+1)}$
* **$\vec{r}(t)$:** $\int \vec{v} dt + \vec{r}_0 = \mathbf{(\frac{5}{6}t^3+2t+5, \frac{1}{6}t^3-2t^2+2, -\frac{1}{6}t^4+t-3)}$

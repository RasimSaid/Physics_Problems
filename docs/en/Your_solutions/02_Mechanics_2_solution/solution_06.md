## 6. Energy Dissipation

**Problem:** Dropped from $2.0 \, \text{m}$, loses 30% energy per bounce. Height after 2nd bounce?

**Solution:**
Energy is proportional to height ($E \propto h$). Retained energy ratio is $0.70$.
* $h_1 = 2.0 \times 0.70 = 1.4 \, \text{m}$
* $h_2 = 1.4 \times 0.70 = \mathbf{0.98 \, \text{m}}$

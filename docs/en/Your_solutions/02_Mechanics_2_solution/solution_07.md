## 7. Dynamics with Friction

**Problem:** $m_1=5 \, \text{kg}$ (tied) on $m_2=10 \, \text{kg}$. Force $F=45 \, \text{N}$ on $m_2$. $\mu_k = 0.2$. Find $a_{10kg}$.

**Solution:**
Friction on $m_2$ comes from two surfaces:
1.  Top ($f_1$): $\mu_k m_1 g = 0.2 \times 5 \times 9.81 = 9.81 \, \text{N}$
2.  Bottom ($f_2$): $\mu_k (m_1+m_2) g = 0.2 \times 15 \times 9.81 = 29.43 \, \text{N}$
$$\sum F = 45 - 9.81 - 29.43 = 5.76 \, \text{N}$$
$$a = \frac{5.76}{10} = \mathbf{0.576 \, \text{m/s}^2}$$

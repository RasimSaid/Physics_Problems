## 12. Work and Energy (Constant Force)

**Given:** $m=2 \, \text{kg}, \vec{F} = [6, 2], \vec{v}_0 = (1, -1)$.

**Solution:**
* **$\vec{a}(t)$:** $(3, 1)$
* **$\vec{v}(t)$:** $(3t+1, t-1)$
* **$\vec{r}(t)$:** $(1.5t^2+t, 0.5t^2-t)$
* **Work at $t=3\text{s}$:** $\Delta \vec{r}(3) = (16.5, 1.5)$. $W = \vec{F} \cdot \Delta \vec{r} = (6 \times 16.5) + (2 \times 1.5) = \mathbf{102 \, \text{J}}$.
* **Theorem Check:** $v(3) = \sqrt{10^2+2^2} = \sqrt{104}$, $v(0) = \sqrt{1^2+(-1)^2} = \sqrt{2}$.
    $\Delta KE = \frac{1}{2}(2)(104-2) = \mathbf{102 \, \text{J}}$. (Matches).

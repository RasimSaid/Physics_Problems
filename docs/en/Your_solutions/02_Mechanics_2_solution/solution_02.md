## 2. Harmonic Motion

**Problem:** $m = 10 \, \text{kg}$, $x(t) = 0.2 \cos(10\pi t)$. Find $k$ and total energy $E$.

**Solution:**
From $x(t) = A \cos(\omega t)$, we identify $A = 0.2 \, \text{m}$ and $\omega = 10\pi \, \text{rad/s}$.

* **Spring Constant ($k$):**
    $$\omega^2 = \frac{k}{m} \implies k = m\omega^2 = 10 \times (10\pi)^2 = 1000\pi^2 \approx \mathbf{9869.6 \, \text{N/m}}$$

* **Total Energy ($E$):**
    $$E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{1}{2}(9869.6)(0.2)^2 = \mathbf{197.39 \, \text{J}}$$

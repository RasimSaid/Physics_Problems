## 5. Inelastic Collision

**Problem:** $70 \, \text{kg}$ runner ($3 \, \text{m/s}$) jumps onto $140 \, \text{kg}$ cart. Final speed? Is $KE$ conserved?

**Solution:**
* **Final Speed:** $m_1v_1 = (m_1 + m_2)v_f \implies 70 \times 3 = 210 \times v_f \implies \mathbf{v_f = 1 \, \text{m/s}}$.
* **Conservation:** **No**, $KE$ is not conserved.
    * $KE_i = \frac{1}{2}(70)(3)^2 = 315 \, \text{J}$
    * $KE_f = \frac{1}{2}(210)(1)^2 = 105 \, \text{J}$
    * Energy is lost to heat/deformation.

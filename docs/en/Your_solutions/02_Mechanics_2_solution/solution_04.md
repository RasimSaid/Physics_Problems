## 4. Energy & Momentum

**Problem:** $0.5 \, \text{kg}$ block drops from $3.0 \, \text{m}$, sticks to $1.5 \, \text{kg}$ block. Find final speed.

**Solution:**
1.  **Speed before collision ($v_1$):** $v_1 = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 9.81 \times 3} \approx 7.67 \, \text{m/s}$.
2.  **Momentum Conservation:** $m_1v_1 = (m_1 + m_2)v_f$.
    $$(0.5)(7.67) = (2.0)v_f \implies v_f = \mathbf{1.92 \, \text{m/s}}$$

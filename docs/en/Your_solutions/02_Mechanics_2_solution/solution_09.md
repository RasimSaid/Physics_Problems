## 9. Vertical Throw with Drag

**Equation:** $m\frac{dv}{dt} = -mg - kv$

**Solution:**
* **Analytical Solution:** $v(t) = (v_0 + \frac{mg}{k})e^{-kt/m} - \frac{mg}{k}$.
* **Max Height:** Set $v(t)=0$ to find $t_{max}$, then integrate $v(t)$ from $0$ to $t_{max}$.
* **Comparison:** Drag results in a **lower** max height and **shorter** ascent time compared to vacuum.

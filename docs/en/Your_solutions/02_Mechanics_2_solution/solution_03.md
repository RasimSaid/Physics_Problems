## 3. Conservation of Energy

**Problem:** $L = 1.0 \, \text{m}$, $\theta = 15^\circ$. Find speed at the bottom.

**Solution:**
Energy conservation: $mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$. The height $h = L(1 - \cos\theta)$.
$$h = 1.0(1 - \cos 15^\circ) \approx 0.0341 \, \text{m}$$
$$v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 9.81 \times 0.0341} \approx \mathbf{0.818 \, \text{m/s}}$$

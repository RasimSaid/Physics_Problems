## 9. Vector Lorentz Force

A proton moves with a velocity  
**v** = (2i - 4j + k) m/s  
in a magnetic field  
**B** = (i + 2j - k) T.  

Find the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the proton.

---

### Step 1: Use the Lorentz Force Formula

The magnetic force is given by:

F = q |v × B|

where:
- q = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (charge of proton)

---

### Step 2: Compute the Cross Product v × B

| i   j   k  |
| 2  -4   1  |
| 1   2  -1  |

v × B = i[(-4)(-1) - (1)(2)]  
       - j[(2)(-1) - (1)(1)]  
       + k[(2)(2) - (-4)(1)]

= i(4 - 2) - j(-2 - 1) + k(4 + 4)  
= 2i + 3j + 8k

---

### Step 3: Find the Magnitude of v × B

|v × B| = √(2² + 3² + 8²)  
= √(4 + 9 + 64)  
= √77

---

### Step 4: Multiply by Charge

F = q |v × B|  
= (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹) × √77  

√77 ≈ 8.77

F ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 8.77  
≈ 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁸ N

---

### Final Answer:

**F ≈ 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁸ N**
